## Identity
- Full name: MIRA Agam Properties
- "Mira" = new chapter, grace. "Agam" (Tamil) = the inner self, home.
- Visual identity inspired by South Indian temple architecture (pillars,
  gateways, vertical rhythm), refined into a modern Dubai symbol. Centerpiece:
  a flame (clarity, auspicious beginnings).
- Tone: boutique, verification-led, discreet, founder-led trust — not a
  high-volume brokerage aesthetic. Copy leans literary/measured, not salesy.
- Tagline options in rotation: "Property, with purpose." / "Rooted in
  heritage. Building legacies."

uses warm off-black and warm off-white, not stark values.

## Typography
- **Display serif (emphasis)**: bold serif caps for the "punch" words in a
  headline (e.g. MILESTONE, COMMITMENT). High contrast against supporting text.
- **Display serif (supporting)**: lighter-weight serif caps for connecting
  headline words (e.g. "A QUIET", "A LIFELONG").
- **Body sans-serif**: clean, minimal sans for paragraph copy and labels.
  Letter-spaced small caps for labels under icons (e.g. "LICENSED IN DUBAI").
- **Script accent**: italic script font used sparingly for a single emotional
  line per poster (closing thought / signature line) — not for body copy.
- Identify and lock the exact typefaces once source design files are
  available; until then, use closely-matched web-safe/Google Fonts serif +
  sans + script combination that mirrors this hierarchy.

## Logo usage
- Lockup: monogram (M with flame accent) stacked above wordmark "MIRA" with
  "AGAM PROPERTIES" in letter-spaced small caps beneath, separated by a small
  gold diamond/rule.
- Always on dark background per current usage. Maintain clear space around
  the monogram — don't crowd it against photo edges or text.

## Recurring motifs (use consistently across posters)
- Thin gold border frame around the full poster canvas.
- Gold diamond (◆) as a section divider, flanked by hairline rules.
- Icon + label rows for trust markers (shield, handshake, person, pin icons
  in gold line-art style), used for credibility points.
- Full-bleed photography (Dubai skyline, architecture) on one side/portion
  of the layout, message block on the other — not photo-behind-full-text.
- Small line-art footer illustration (e.g. palm trees / low-rise skyline) as
  a grounding motif at the base of the poster.
- CTA presented as a bordered bar/button, not a floating text line.

## Poster generation rules (for the social-assistant module)
1. Every poster must include the monogram+wordmark lockup — never omit it.
2. Stick to the palette above — no off-brand colors introduced by generation.
3. One emotional/script-font line per poster maximum — this is an accent,
   not a repeated device.
4. New-listing posters should be data-driven: pull price/beds/baths/community
   name/developer partner from the listing record, not free-text prompted.
5. Distinguish own-brand content from developer-co-branded content (DAMAC/
   Sobha logos, if used, follow each developer's own brand guidelines — check
   before combining marks; some developers restrict how their marks are used
   alongside partner branding).
6. All output drafts to the review queue — no auto-publish, per SPEC.md.
